Description

Short

UMBRA: ENDLESS is a dark-fantasy survival roguelite made for the Apple Watch first and at home on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Steer through a dying cosmic kingdom while your weapons strike on their own, then choose upgrades, evolve your weapons and face the bosses waiting in the dark.

Long

A dying cosmic kingdom where time has fractured. The player is an immortal relic bearer trapped between collapsing dimensions, and every expedition goes deeper into a shifting nightmare of corrupted knights, spectral creatures, eldritch machines and ancient gods.

The player steers and the weapons attack on their own: on Apple Watch with the Digital Crown, on iPhone and iPad by dragging anywhere on the screen, and on Mac with the keyboard, the pointer or a game controller. Each level-up offers three upgrades. Weapons evolve, tags combine into synergies, and twelve bosses each announce their attacks before they land.

Runs last five, twelve or twenty minutes, or never end in The Endless Abyss. A campaign opens the eight biomes one by one, a Daily Rite brings a new challenge every day, and Boss Trials pit the player against a single boss in its home arena.

One purchase covers all four devices. Progress and runs in progress travel between them through the player's own iCloud, so a run paused on the wrist can be continued on a phone or a Mac. The game works fully offline and collects no data.

Key features

  • Six heroes, each with its own weapon and passive: Ash Knight, Hex Weaver, Void Ranger, Blood Oracle, Grave Warden and Chronomancer.
  • 30 weapons and 30 evolutions, more than 60 relics that change the rules, 12 bosses and 8 biomes.
  • Quick Hunt, Standard and Deep Expeditions, The Endless Abyss, Boss Trials, a campaign and the Daily Rite, with 15 ascension levels.
  • Every picture and every sound generated by the game's own code, written in Rust: no pre-drawn sprites, no recorded samples.
  • One purchase for Apple Watch, iPhone, iPad and Mac. No ads, no in-app purchases.
  • iCloud saves across your devices, and 79 Game Center achievements.
  • Accessibility options: reduce motion, high contrast, colour-vision modes, text sizes, simpler effects, gore levels and gameplay assists.
