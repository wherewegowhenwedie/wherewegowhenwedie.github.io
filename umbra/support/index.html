Questions and answers

Do I need iCloud or an internet connection to play?

No. The game works fully offline on every device. iCloud and Game Center are both optional.

Where are my saves, and how does iCloud sync work?

Your progress, settings and any run in progress are saved on each device. If you are signed in to iCloud and leave iCloud on for the game, a copy also goes to your own private iCloud, and every device signed in to the same Apple Account shares it: your progress merges across them, and a run you pause on one device can be continued on another.

  • Pause before switching devices. If a device goes offline or its battery runs out in the middle of a run, the part of the run played since that device last uploaded may be missing on the other device: usually up to about two minutes, more in Low Power Mode or while it was offline. The game warns you before you continue an older copy.
  • When iCloud is full, signed out, turned off for the game or temporarily unavailable, the game tells you and you keep playing on the device; it syncs again later.
  • If a different Apple Account signs in, sync stops and asks what to do. Progress never moves from one Apple Account to another without your answer.

Do the Apple Watch and the iPhone share progress?

Yes, through iCloud: sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both and leave iCloud on for the game. They do not need to be near each other: each syncs with your iCloud when it can. The same goes for your iPad and Mac.

I have a new device. How do I keep my progress?

Before you change devices, open the game on the old one with iCloud on, so its latest progress is uploaded. Then install the game on the new device, sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, and open it: your progress comes down from iCloud. Without iCloud, saves stay only on the device where you played them.

How do I delete my game data from iCloud?

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete iCloud data, then confirm. This deletes the game's data from your iCloud and turns sync off on that device. The progress on the device itself stays; if you turn sync on again later, that device's progress is uploaded again. Your other devices notice the deletion and ask you before uploading anything again.

To start over instead, Reset progress (also in Settings, also confirmed) erases your progress. With iCloud on, your other devices start over too.

If the game is no longer on any of your devices, delete its data from your iCloud storage settings instead: see how long we keep things in the Privacy Policy.

What accessibility settings are there?

In the game's Settings:

  • Reduce motion: follows your device's Reduce Motion setting (System), or On or Off.
  • High contrast: darker ground, thick outlines and a bright hero.
  • Colour vision: Standard, Red-green or Blue-yellow (hatched danger zones, double edges).
  • Text size: Normal, Large or Largest.
  • Simple effects: fewer flashes and particles.
  • Gore: Full, Muted or Off. Damage numbers: Auto (set for your screen), All, Crits (critical and elite hits) or Off.
  • Haptics: On, Essential (major alerts only) or Off, and five volume controls: Master, Music, Effects, Ambience and Interface.
  • Difficulty (Relaxed, Normal, Hard) and assists: Avoid assist, Auto-dash, Grace frames and Move speed.
  • On Apple Watch: Reverse steer and the Crown's speed and steering style.

On first launch, the game also picks up your device's Increase Contrast, Dim Flashing Lights, text size and Bold Text settings. VoiceOver is not supported at launch.

Does one purchase cover all my devices?

Yes. One purchase of USD 9.99 (or the equivalent in your country's App Store) covers Apple Watch, iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, and Apple handles refunds; we cannot issue them ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you bought the game with.

Do I need Game Center?

No. If you are signed in to Game Center, you can earn 79 achievements there. Game Center is optional: the game never requires you to sign in. You can dismiss Game Center's sign-in, and everything works without it.

Supported devices

The requirements planned for launch. The App Store page shows the final ones.

Apple Watch

watchOS 27 or later.

  • Apple Watch SE (3rd generation)
  • Apple Watch Series 9 and later
  • Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later

iPhone

iOS 15 or later, with an A12 Bionic chip or newer.

  • iPhone XS, iPhone XS Max, iPhone XR and every later iPhone
  • iPhone SE (2nd generation) and later

iPad

iPadOS 15 or later, with an A12 chip or newer.

  • iPad (8th generation) and later
  • iPad mini (5th generation) and later
  • iPad Air (3rd generation) and later
  • iPad Pro 11-inch (every generation), iPad Pro 12.9-inch (3rd generation) and later, iPad Pro 13-inch

Mac

macOS 12 Monterey or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon.

  • Intel-based Macs are not supported at launch.
