The game

A dying cosmic kingdom where time has fractured. You are an immortal relic bearer trapped between collapsing dimensions, and every expedition goes deeper into a shifting nightmare of corrupted knights, spectral creatures, eldritch machines and ancient gods.

You choose where to go; your weapons attack on their own. Kill to gain experience, pick one of three upgrades at each level, discover weapon evolutions and synergies, and survive the bosses waiting at the end of each hunt. Every run is different: the world, the enemies and the upgrades you are offered are generated fresh each time.

Every picture and every sound in the game is generated by its own code as you play: no pre-drawn sprites, no recorded samples.

Six heroes

  • Ash Knight

    Close range and heavy armour: circular attacks, and retaliation when you are hit.

  • Hex Weaver

    Curses, chains and control of the space around you.

  • Void Ranger

    Mobile and precise: piercing projectiles and critical hits.

  • Blood Oracle

    Trades health for power that keeps on rising.

  • Grave Warden

    Summons the dead to fight beside you and holds the ground.

  • Chronomancer

    Bends time: delayed strikes, shorter cooldowns, slower enemies.

Built for every device you own

    Made for the wrist first. Steer with the Digital Crown, tap to dash. Plays on the watch on its own.

  • Mac

    A native Mac app: keyboard, mouse or trackpad, and game controllers. The hero follows your pointer; Space or a click dashes.

Your saves, on all your devices

With iCloud on, your progress follows your Apple Account: pause a run on your watch and continue it on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Saves go to your own private iCloud, which we cannot see.

iCloud is optional. The game works fully offline, and without iCloud your saves simply stay on each device.

Privacy Policy: Data Not Collected

Game Center

79 achievements to earn, from your first steps to the deepest dives. Game Center is optional: the game never requires you to sign in. You can dismiss Game Center's sign-in, and everything works without it.

Accessibility

  • Reduce motion, which follows your device's Reduce Motion setting unless you choose otherwise.
  • High contrast: darker ground, thick outlines and a bright hero.
  • Colour-vision modes for red-green and blue-yellow colour blindness: hatched danger zones and double edges.
  • Three text sizes, and "Simple effects" for fewer flashes and particles.
  • On first launch, the game picks up your device's Increase Contrast, Dim Flashing Lights, text size and Bold Text settings.
  • Relaxed difficulty and assists: avoid assist, auto-dash, grace after a dash, and an adjustable move speed.
  • Gore levels, damage numbers, haptic strength and five separate volume controls.
