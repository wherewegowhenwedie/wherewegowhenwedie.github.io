UMBRA: ENDLESS

Privacy Policy

UMBRA: ENDLESS collects no data. Nothing about you or the way you play is sent to us.

Effective date: 25 November 2026

UMBRA: ENDLESS is coming soon. This policy describes the game as it will be at launch.

Who we are

UMBRA: ENDLESS is made by Where We Go When We Die, an independent game studio. On the App Store, the seller is listed under the developer's own name. This policy covers the game on Apple Watch, iPhone, iPad and Mac, and this website.

The studio's founder is responsible for privacy and the protection of personal information. You can reach them at support@wherewegowhenwedie.dev.

In short

  • The game collects no data. Its App Store privacy details are "Data Not Collected".
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts of our own, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your saves stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • iCloud and Game Center are optional services run by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The only personal information that reaches us is what you send us by email.

What stays on your device

Your progress, your settings and any run in progress are saved in files inside the game's own storage on each device. To keep saves from different devices apart, the game also creates a random identifier for each device; that file stays on the device and, on Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad, is excluded from device backups. On a Mac, the game also remembers the size and position of its window.

If you play with head-tracking headphones on iPhone, iPad or Mac, the game uses your head's movement to place sounds around you. This motion data stays on your device.

None of this is sent to us.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and leave iCloud on for the game, the game keeps a copy of your game data in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so that you can continue on your other devices. That copy holds:

  • your progress, the settings that follow you between devices, and your runs in progress;
  • what the game needs to merge copies from several devices: each device's random identifier and its model (for example "Watch7,3"), plus version numbers and counters that put changes in order.

This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account. Only you can access your private iCloud database: we, the developer, cannot see, read or download it. Your Apple Account's name and identifier are never written into it; on your device, the game keeps only a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of your iCloud user identifier, to tell accounts apart.

You stay in control:

  • Without iCloud, or with iCloud turned off for the game, everything stays on the device.
  • In the game's Settings, Delete iCloud data deletes the game's data from your iCloud and turns sync off on that device.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports the achievements you earn to Apple's Game Center. Game Center is Apple's service, provided under Apple's privacy policy. It is optional: the game never requires you to sign in. You can dismiss Game Center's sign-in, and the game works fully without it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, and no tracking of you across apps or websites.
  • No accounts of our own: nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No third-party SDKs or libraries that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • We never sell or share data, because we do not have any.

The game makes no network connections of its own: it reaches only Apple's iCloud and Game Center, through Apple's own frameworks, and only if you use them.

Children

UMBRA: ENDLESS is not made for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and to help with your request, and for no other purpose. In European terms, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering a request you chose to send us. We never sell or share them, and never use them for marketing.

Mail to our support address goes through Cloudflare Email Routing, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., which forwards it to the mailbox where we read it, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. These services may handle your email in the United States or other countries.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., which may record technical information, such as visitors' IP addresses, for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those records.

How long we keep things, and your rights

  • Support emails are kept only as long as we need them to help you, and deleted at the latest 12 months after our last exchange.
  • On your devices, the game's data stays until you delete it. Deleting the game from an Apple Watch, iPhone or iPad deletes everything it saved there. On a Mac, macOS keeps an app's data after the app is deleted: to remove it, also delete the game's folder, ~/Library/Containers/dev.wherewegowhenwedie.umbraendless.
  • In your iCloud, the game's copy stays until you delete it, even after the game itself is deleted. In the game, open Settings and choose Delete iCloud data. Without the game: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, Manage Account Storage (Manage Storage on older systems), UMBRA: ENDLESS and Delete Data from iCloud; on a Mac, open System Settings (System Preferences on older systems), click your name, then iCloud and Manage. The names can differ slightly between system versions. We cannot access this copy, so only you can delete it.

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you (in practice, only your emails to us), ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to support@wherewegowhenwedie.dev; we answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to a data protection authority: in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information; in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of the game were ever to collect any data, this policy and the game's App Store privacy details would be updated before that version is released.
